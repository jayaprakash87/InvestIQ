# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Assume good faith and critique work rather than people. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, or disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Report incidents privately to project maintainers through GitHub's repository contact or reporting mechanisms rather than public issues. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation after reviewing reports. We will strive for fair, proportionate and confidential handling.
