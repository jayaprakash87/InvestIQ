# Thesis challenge

## Inputs
One or more venue-qualified instruments, thesis statement, assumption IDs, a research cutoff and supporting/contradicting source observations.

## Procedure
1. Restate the thesis as testable business and valuation hypotheses, not as a prediction of certain returns.
2. Distinguish what has direct factual support, what is externally estimated, what is subjective assumption and what remains unknown.
3. Identify the strongest plausible disconfirming mechanisms (competition, regulation, capacity, cyclicality, financing, dilution, accounting or demand).
4. Propose falsifiable checkpoints, observable metrics, future events and conditions that would force a thesis revision.
5. Stress-test how scenario assumptions affect outcomes; request missing evidence rather than inventing a negative or positive narrative.

## Output
Use `ResearchReport` with `skill="thesis-challenge"`, explicit `inference`, `assumption`, and `data_gap` findings, and unanswered verification questions.

## Safeguards
Do not manufacture contradictory sources. Do not assert access to undisclosed financial information or promise investment outperformance. The goal is research robustness, not persuasive confidence.
